Everything a semester throws at you

Each part of StudySync works on its own and feeds the same dashboard and calendar.

Assignments and exams

Record deadlines, priorities and progress. Filter and sort to see what matters first.

Weekly timetable

Lay out your classes by day and time. Overlapping classes are caught before they are saved.

One calendar

See assignments, exams, classes, study sessions and personal events together.

Deadline reminders

In-app notifications for upcoming and overdue work, with your own reminder times.

Pomodoro timer

Focus and break cycles you can customise. Finished sessions are saved to your history.

Study progress

Charts of your study hours and consistency, based on the sessions you actually record.

GPA and CGPA

Calculate semester GPA and cumulative CGPA from credit hours and grades.
